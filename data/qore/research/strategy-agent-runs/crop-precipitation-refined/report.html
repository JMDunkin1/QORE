Fallback mechanics

On idle days the strategy is 100% invested in the 80% VOO / 20% QQQM basket. A 35% crop position leaves 65% in that basket. Daily return is the proportional index return plus the signed crop return, less crop-position turnover cost; the basket itself keeps the all-year strategy’s costless daily-target-weight assumption.
