Method and rejection gates

What the screen does

  • Builds 7/14/30/60-day precipitation anomalies against a frozen 2000–2010 seasonal baseline.
  • Applies crop- and stage-specific dry, wet, or two-sided supply-stress logic.
  • Trades only from the close after a two-calendar-day observation lag, then charges 5 bps per side.
  • Selects each crop rule on 2011–2017 training plus 2018–2021 validation only.
  • Reports 2022 onward once, then adjusts holdout p-values across crop winners with Benjamini–Hochberg.

What must happen next

  • Replace finalized precipitation with archived operational GEFS forecast vintages and explicit availability timestamps.
  • Replace Yahoo continuous proxies with individual contracts, fixed rolls, collateral yield, and tick-level cost scenarios.
  • Add prior-year production weights, NASS crop-stage releases, soil moisture, irrigation masks, and evapotranspiration.
  • Bootstrap by synchronized crop year; treat annual seasons—not daily rows—as the independent sample.
  • Keep only A/B lanes for prospectively frozen paper monitoring.
