Photo translated into testable regimes

Long crop-futures overlay

  • Hot and dry: below-normal rolling precipitation plus above-normal rolling maximum temperature across at least 18% of active production weight.
  • Drought: precipitation at least 1.5 times beyond the candidate dry threshold.

Short crop-futures overlay

  • Cool and wet: above-normal precipitation plus below-normal mean temperature.

What changed

  • Only soybeans and corn remain active.
  • Stress is classified at each site before aggregation, so dry U.S. weather is not canceled by wet South American weather.
  • Signals are limited to fixed reproductive/fill windows and separate U.S., Brazil, Argentina, and global lanes.
  • Only four atomic photo families remain. Duplicate direction vectors are removed before selection.
  • The basket stays fully invested and crop futures are capped at a fixed 35% notional overlay.

Walk-forward integrity

  • Each outer test year sees only the preceding ten years.
  • Candidate ranking uses only the trailing five inner-validation years, requires three of five positive relative years, and survives 20 bps one-way costs.
  • The ensemble may hold zero to three behaviorally distinct families; no passing candidate means zero crop exposure.
  • Before QQQM exists, 80% SPY / 20% QQQ is labeled as a research proxy. The recent period uses the exact 80% VOO / 20% QQQM fallback.
  • Five basis points one-way crop turnover cost and a two-calendar-day signal delay are applied.
