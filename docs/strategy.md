# All-Year Natural-Gas Strategy Contract

## Scope

`ngas-all-year-beta` is QORE's only public strategy. It is a deterministic daily selector over two internal component ledgers:

1. use the material Summer Alpha row;
2. otherwise use the material Winter Alpha row;
3. otherwise use the shared `US-INDEX-BASKET` fallback target.

A component row is material when it has a non-index thesis, non-zero gas position, or an explicit index-to-cash allocation change. If summer and winter are both material on the same date, artifact generation fails instead of choosing silently. The all-year layer selects targets first and then recomputes the complete UNG/VOO/QQQM execution ledger, so component-local costs cannot decide which lane wins. It adds no threshold search, sizing search, or holdout-driven selection.

## Economic thesis

The winter engine tests the observation that sufficiently broad cold forecasts across the central and eastern United States can raise expected heating demand and natural-gas prices. Longer-lead cold confirmation can support a gas-follow position; as the event approaches or the market overreacts, the engine can use weather resolution and same-direction price behavior for a reversion/fade decision.

The summer engine applies the same demand logic to cooling: fresh, multi-model heat forecasts can support a gas-long overlay, while same-direction rallies can qualify for a storage- and demand-aware fade. These are tested rules, not assumed causal facts.

## Timing and inputs

- Forecast issue-date signals execute only on later trading sessions.
- Summer live target inference requires a complete common NOAA GFS/GEFS 00z issue set. Winter signal scoring uses GFS, GEFS mean, GraphCastGFS, and AIGFS, while the reviewed heating-demand context can also use ECMWF IFS, ECMWF AIFS, and GEM Global. Selected source lists are derived from the frozen component contract; stale, incomplete, or substituted forecast state fails closed.
- Summer research coverage requires the exact reviewed 18-location eastern-CONUS demand basket and its 1.06 total weight on every active-source issue date. The IDs and weights are versioned once in `scripts/lib/qore-summer-location-universe.mjs`, mirror `data/qore/weather/locations.csv`, and are embedded in the canonical live component contract; a missing, duplicate, unexpected, or reweighted location fails that issue date and blocks component promotion.
- Forecast location rows must contain finite forecast, normal, and anomaly temperatures; forecast and normal means must remain inside the deliberately broad -150F to 160F physical range, and the stated anomaly must agree with forecast minus normal within 0.01F. A failure at any location invalidates the complete source/issue/target/lead atom. Live target inference uses reject mode and fails closed before scoring. Calendar construction, historical optimization, weather QA, and parity replay use quarantine mode, removing both the atom's complete location set and matching score row while recording diagnostics in generated artifacts. Partial use of a corrupt atom is forbidden.
- QORE's executable spatial representation is the versioned 18-location load-weighted basket and its low-dimensional anomaly, breadth, HDD, and CDD summaries. A freeze-off proxy or other physical load summary may be evaluated only as a separately frozen research shadow; raster maps, CNNs, and other high-capacity spatial models are not part of the reviewed signal contract.
- Live price inputs come from an ignored rolling Yahoo cache of completed sessions. The target-date daily bar is excluded; both gas series must cover the authoritative VOO/QQQM session set, and an unverified intervening weekday fails closed.
- Open-Meteo current forecasts provide operational weather/risk context; they do not set the broker target.
- EIA Lower 48 storage is used only after its versioned WNGSR publication timestamp.
- NASA POWER actual temperatures and 1991–2020 normals support historical anomaly and forecast-quality work.
- Unallocated deployable capital follows the configured VOO/QQQM target-weight index basket. The frozen research execution contract keeps the broker's default 2% cash buffer uninvested.

The exact frozen thresholds, sources, sizing rules, costs, and split dates are authoritative in the component `run-summary.json` files and the executable live contract consumed by `scripts/lib/qore-live-all-year-inference.mjs`. The all-year artifact stores their canonical contract and digest; paper/live inference fails closed unless that reviewed digest matches the executable contract. The same seal includes the canonical Alpaca execution profile from `config/qore-live-broker-settings.json` and the versioned index basket: deployment/cash buffer, deadband, order mechanics, short policy, data feed, quote/clock/risk limits, and request timeout. Production paper/live routing resolves those values through the same parser and blocks before broker access if an environment override changes the reviewed digest. Change those together; do not describe one contract and execute another.

## Research contract

The summer and winter components retain their own train, validation, and holdout boundaries. Each component selects and determines its eligibility on its own declared train/validation period only. The all-year artifact records each source row's component split separately. Its public `split` is one calendar-wide partition: train ends at the earliest component train end, public holdout starts at the latest component holdout, and the intervening sessions are validation. A separate selection cutoff ends before the earliest component holdout. All-year return-based promotion gates and the selection bootstrap use only that chronological prefix; later composite returns are reporting-only at the all-year layer, while the component-eligibility gates retain each component's declared pre-holdout result. This ensures no component validation row is mislabeled as public holdout and no component holdout return enters the current all-year return-based gate calculation. It does not make those historical periods pristine after repeated strategy development.

All-year eligibility uses a centered circular block bootstrap of every chronological daily active return in that contiguous calendar-wide train/validation prefix versus the index fallback. The UI's Monte Carlo panel is explicitly a report-only full-calendar block-bootstrap diagnostic, not a forecast of live wealth or an independent proof of significance.

## Validation integrity seal

`config/qore-validation-integrity.json` is the reviewed, versioned anti-overfit boundary. It records that all evidence from 2021 through the currently observed 2026 data is development-contaminated: strategy development began on 2026-06-11, data was observed through 2026-07-14, and untouched prospective collection starts on 2026-07-23. Historical train, validation, and holdout labels remain useful for reproducible diagnostics, but none is claimed as pristine out-of-sample evidence.

The seal is bound to an end-to-end strategy-contract digest covering the component signal/source/sizing contract, exact Summer location universe, all-year selector, production-source target-parity policy, research execution, reviewed broker execution/risk profile, and overnight policy. It separately seals a canonical all-year artifact core containing the stable contracts, selected results, parity evidence, static gates, and byte/header/row/date bindings for both `selected-trades.csv` and `display-curve.csv`, while excluding generated timestamps, derived status, and mutable prospective evidence/approval fields. Runtime verifies those two ledgers and their common chronological date grid; the Backtest adapter also rejects a display curve whose SHA-256 or shape does not match the reviewed summary. This fixed-point core prevents the seal from becoming cyclic while still making a material artifact mutation invalidate routing. A contract or artifact-core change invalidates the seal; prior prospective observations cannot be reused for the changed strategy. Every forward record is captured before the reviewed NYSE session opens and individually binds its canonical inference digest to the sealed strategy-contract and strategy-artifact digests. Runtime derives independent episodes only from explicit, causal, non-overlapping forecast/observation windows. It derives a complete Summer or Winter only when the artifact contains every reviewed NYSE session in that season; neither rows nor counts declared by the artifact are trusted. Future-dated evidence or approvals and same-day period-end reviews fail against the runtime validation cutoff. Live eligibility requires at least 60 independent prospective weather episodes, two complete prospective summers, two complete prospective winters, `pristineForwardEvidence: true`, and explicit live approval bound to the exact sealed strategy and broker profile.

The separately digested Summer fade-ramp challenger is research-only shadow logic: no fade below a 1.50% realized same-direction move, linearly increasing size from zero at 1.50% to the existing reviewed fade size at 2.00%, and unchanged full size above 2.00%. Its frozen comparator embeds and digests the complete active Summer component contract, including signal parameters, implementation rules, position caps, and exact location universe. It is not part of active candidate selection, the executable component contract, a broker handoff, or either promotion gate. Shadow targets are append-only prospective observations beginning 2026-07-23 and are accepted only on the active Summer comparator schedule (including its April/May boundary), before 09:30 New York time on a reviewed NYSE session. The frozen calendar contains NYSE's published 2026-2028 full-day closures; Winter-only sessions, weekends, closures, and dates outside that reviewed horizon fail closed. Each record validates both target projections, allocations, identifiers, strategy/artifact digests, and input-provenance digests before its first permanent write. Targets cannot be backfilled or relabeled as pristine evidence for a later changed executable contract.

Paper eligibility has its own explicit approval and exact contract-seal gate so paper evidence can be collected before the live evidence gate passes. Real-capital eligibility additionally requires reviewed paper execution evidence bound to the sealed strategy and broker profile and a server-only pseudonymized account: at least 60 trading sessions and 10 fills, including at least four UNG fills with two long and two short, with median absolute slippage no more than 25 bps and p95 absolute slippage no more than 50 bps. The paper session list must be the complete reviewed NYSE session set between its endpoints. Each order binds a canonical broker-record digest; each fill carries coherent side, quantity, and pre/post signed positions plus a fresh timestamped bid/ask midpoint. Directional UNG counts derive from those broker fields rather than a declared target direction. Forward and paper evidence use schema-versioned JSON contracts at fixed paths under `.local/qore/validation-evidence/`; they must be owner-only regular files rather than symlinks. Runtime recomputes each raw-file SHA-256, derives all forward and paper summary fields, and requires paper/live approvals to match the exact sealed strategy-contract and broker-profile digests before a gate can pass. Absent approvals must carry null digest bindings. The account pseudonym remains only in the protected paper artifact and server-side manifest and is not projected into the browser-imported research artifact. The evidence period, evidence digest, review timestamp, runtime cutoff, and paper/live approval ordering all fail closed. Checked-in artifact overrides require an explicit test capability and cannot reach real Alpaca endpoints. The checked-in manifest starts all forward and paper-execution counts at zero and records both approvals as absent, so both paper and live routing remain disabled. The manifest digest and public-safe material fields are embedded in the all-year artifact and every live inference handoff; editing the manifest alone is insufficient because the artifact must be regenerated and reviewed. The legacy `promotionEligible` handoff field is retained as an alias for `liveEligible`, never for paper eligibility.

Run the pipeline with:

```bash
npm run train
npm run backtest
```

The authoritative output is `data/qore/research/strategy-agent-runs/ngas-all-year-beta/run-summary.json`; `selected-trades.csv` is the full audit ledger and `display-curve.csv` is its narrow UI projection.

## Research versus execution

Summer Alpha retains Yahoo's continuous `NG=F` proxy only for price confirmation, realized-move gates, and signal-volatility sizing. Every gas return used for Summer selection, Winter selection, and the all-year portfolio is Yahoo `UNG` ETF history. The all-year ledger therefore has one gas P&L contract: `UNG`, matching the Alpaca adapter.

The frozen `config/qore-research-execution.json` contract applies a causal daily timeline. Prior-close holdings earn the close-to-open move; current targets become effective at the split- and distribution-adjusted session open and earn only open-to-close returns. Any same-date close-in weather update used at that open comes from a frozen 00Z run assumed available before the New York session; the historical calendar does not store a separate publication timestamp. Summer and Winter storage logic uses the versioned EIA WNGSR release calendar, including published holiday changes and documented extraordinary delays, and admits a report only at a session open after its publication timestamp. Winter season drawdown uses September as its canonical accumulation boundary. A missing storage release-calendar row fails closed. NASA POWER actual meteorology carries a conservative three-calendar-day availability lag. Turnover is computed separately for UNG, VOO, and QQQM after drift and the broker's default 0.25% rebalance deadband. That deadband may suppress immaterial same-direction drift, but the shared policy always executes UNG entry, exit, direction-change, and target risk-reduction orders; it also carries paired asset reductions with any executable gross increase and forces reductions when the portfolio starts above its deployment envelope. The Alpaca reconciler applies the same rule again at its fresh-quote boundary and blocks if excess exposure cannot be reduced by a valid order. Exactly one frozen baseline cost scenario may rank candidates; elevated and stress costs are reporting-only and cannot be made selection-eligible without changing the versioned contract.

The separate `config/qore-overnight-risk-policy.json` contract tests whether QORE should reduce UNG at the close and restore the ordinary target at the next open. Its 50-policy audit covers fixed retention, long-only and short-only flattening, weekend flattening, and lagged rolling-gap guards under baseline, elevated, and stress friction. Calendar years 2021–2023 train the overlay, 2024 validates it, and 2025 onward is a report-only retrospective period. The pre-2025 winner is the sole research recommendation; later comparisons cannot promote, reject, or replace it. The deployed policy remains `carry-100` until the recommended close-side execution path is separately reviewed and implemented. Reproduce the audit with `npm run research:overnight-risk`.

`carry-100` means no closing-auction or overnight-session order is added: the prior-close UNG holding owns the overnight move and the next causal target still starts at the regular-session open. Alternative auction or extended-hours routes remain research-only unless their own timestamped quote/fill evidence, broker entitlement, order cutoffs, and whole-share constraints are validated.

This is still an idealized once-per-session execution simulation. The live supervisor may reassess an updated target or drift intraday, while the daily Yahoo history cannot reconstruct those quote crossings or fills. Missing historical quotes and fills cannot be recreated, and future gaps, slippage, partial fills, market impact, and borrow availability remain live outcomes. NASA POWER actuals are not archived as immutable historical vintages, so later upstream revisions also cannot be removed retrospectively. ETF expenses and historical futures-roll tracking are already embedded in UNG adjusted prices; taxes remain account-specific and are excluded from the pretax research return.

Negative `UNG` targets require a margin/short-capable Alpaca account, explicit QORE permission, current Alpaca shortability, and whole-share sizing. If those conditions fail, QORE blocks the order rather than changing the strategy.

True `NG`, `MNG`, or `QG` execution remains out of scope until the project has reviewed contract-month selection, expiry and roll logic, margin and price-limit handling, and delivery-risk controls.

## Promotion standard

Treat the checked-in artifact as a research baseline. Paper routing requires paper eligibility; live routing additionally requires pristine prospective evidence and explicit live approval. The production-source replay matches all 585 Summer targets whose weather target falls in May-September—including seven material April entries for May targets—and all 1,362 Winter targets. Combined parity is therefore 1,947/1,947 across gas position, index fraction, and thesis. Runtime recomputes and content-binds both component replays through the same Winter target engine used by research and live inference instead of trusting the generated gate boolean. Parity alone is not promotion: the selection-prefix bootstrap, train drawdown, component, prospective-evidence, paper-execution, and approval gates remain authoritative. Before enabling real capital, also require clean reproducible artifacts, passing inference parity and trading tests, non-overlapping Alpaca paper evidence, reviewed broker/risk configuration, a clean deployed commit, and a successful no-order readiness audit. Live account performance must be evaluated from Alpaca telemetry, never spliced with the research curve.
