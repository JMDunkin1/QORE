# NGAS All-Year Beta strategy audit — 2026-07-21

## Decision

Do not promote a return-optimized historical variant. Keep `ngas-all-year-beta` in `needs-validation` and keep paper/live routing disabled. No tested return change could be defended below the requested 33% overfit-probability ceiling; the best low-risk idea is frozen as a non-executable prospective shadow instead.

The checked-in strategy has useful development evidence, but it does not satisfy a defensible non-overfit promotion standard. Its pre-holdout bootstrap is not significant, its train drawdown breaches the promotion floor, and the Winter component has no eligible candidate. More importantly, repository development began after the dates labeled as component holdouts, and holdout results were printed throughout repeated searches. The 2021-2026 history must therefore be treated as development-contaminated retrospective evidence rather than sealed out-of-sample evidence.

The 2026-07-22 second pass reached the same decision after testing source-universe stationarity, independent weather-episode attribution, physical weather-data integrity, execution-cost sensitivity, and spatial-demand extensions. The only new active behavior is input hardening: physically impossible temperature atoms now fail closed in live inference and are quarantined reproducibly in historical research. No return-optimized signal or sizing change is promoted.

## What was tested

- Reconstructed the search history from Git. The recorded Summer family grew from 27,648 to 103,680 candidates and the Winter family from 10 to 642 before later narrowing. Reported holdout metrics remained visible while those families changed.
- Re-ran the current 2-candidate Summer family and the current 135-candidate Winter family.
- Re-expanded Summer to its 103,680-variant grid under the current causal UNG execution accounting. Of 590 mechanically eligible rows, the strongest improved headline return and drawdown but had a selection-adjusted p-value of 0.0758 even though the adjustment retained only 80 surviving near-top candidates rather than the complete historical search universe.
- Re-attributed each Summer leg against an otherwise identical index-fallback ledger and clustered results by independent issue-date episode, rather than crediting blended portfolio returns or repeated holding days as independent observations.
- Ran an isolated broad Winter neighborhood of 2,025 combinations across hold policy, repeat-signal freshness, storage gate, heating-demand sizing, and 1.00x-1.25x overlay risk.
- Re-tested a deliberately simpler Winter challenger with a three-day repeated-signal freshness rule and 1.00x overlay risk.
- Replayed a fixed, full-history GFS/GEFS Winter source core under equal and train-fitted weights, together with adjacent source/demand variants, through the exact all-year execution engine. This tested whether the expanding source universe was creating a non-stationary score rather than adding stable information.
- Tested hard Summer realized-move thresholds from 1.00% through 5.00%, then a theory-first continuous fade ramp that scales from zero at a 1.50% move to the existing full fade size at 2.00%. Replayed both through the exact all-year execution engine.
- Tested the continuous ramp under baseline, elevated, and stress costs, inspected every changed target and its subsequent overnight window, and compared it with a broader 1.50%-4.00% ramp to check whether the result generalized beyond the three threshold-edge events.
- Tested all 50 versioned overnight policies under baseline, elevated, and stress friction. `carry-100` remains the correct deployed policy; the close-side challenger failed the holdout comparisons and lacks executable fill evidence.
- Swept wider broker/research rebalance deadbands, first naively and then with mandatory UNG entry/exit/sign-flip, risk-reduction, and over-deployment semantics. The apparent benefit disappeared once the test obeyed the trading-safety contract.
- Checked baseline/elevated/stress transaction costs and borrow assumptions.
- Audited every versioned forecast-location row for physical plausibility and anomaly consistency before recomputing weather QA, research inputs, and production-source parity.
- Assessed the proposed volumetric-weather-map direction. QORE already uses an 18-location, load-weighted eastern-CONUS spatial approximation; the evidence supports testing a few preregistered physical summaries, not fitting an image model to this small episode count.
- Audited signal timing, split boundaries, forecast coverage, live-source parity, execution-price parity, artifact promotion, broker provenance, and exposure limits.
- Examined block-bootstrap sensitivity. On the all-year selection prefix, p-values ranged from 0.192 at a one-session block to 0.034 at a 252-session block; the fixed ten-session result is about 0.15. That instability is inconsistent with a strong promotion claim.

The hard 1.50% Summer threshold raised the exact all-year full return from 293.56% to 308.15% in the pre-remediation audit run, but it changed only three dates and the selection-prefix bootstrap remained weak at about p=0.125. The continuous ramp was safer: it raised that run to 301.15%, with a 28.60% CAGR, 1.324 Sharpe, and the same drawdown. It also survived stress costs. However, two pre-selection overnight gaps produced nearly all of that gain, the largest incremental UNG target was still 32.38% of deployable capital, and the unadjusted selection-prefix p-value remained about 0.134. A broader 1.50%-4.00% ramp lost to the current strategy. The narrow ramp therefore remains a prospective shadow, not an executed target.

The independent-episode Summer attribution is more informative than the headline component return. Heat-follow signals added 16.32 percentage points versus an identical index fallback across 52 independent issue episodes, but t=0.87 and the leg was negative in both 2023 (-2.93 points) and 2024 (-2.03 points). The fade leg added 21.73 points across 42 episodes with t=2.29 and positive marginal results in 2023, 2024, and 2025 (+6.85, +7.58, and +7.31 points). The physically appealing extreme-CDD subset showed +17.98 points across 13 episodes, t=3.03, and an 84.6% win rate, but it is tiny and was identified after observing the data. Replacing the existing trigger with that CDD rule reduced holdout edge to +2.49 points. The result supports prospective low-dimensional demand-feature research, not a historical trigger replacement.

The strongest second-pass Winter headline came from fixing both signal and heating-demand inputs to GFS/GEFS and weighting them equally. It raised the all-year full return from 293.02% to 318.00% and Sharpe from 1.307 to 1.375, while slightly worsening maximum drawdown from -28.34% to -28.71%. The chronological evidence rejects promotion: incremental net return was +6.936 points in training through 2024-03-31, -2.372 points in validation, +1.309 points in holdout, and -1.063 points across the combined post-train period; the latter was about 2.41 compounded-return points worse than the active comparator. Only 9 of 21 changed episodes were positive, the best episode supplied 80.9% of the total gain, and removing the top three changed +5.873 points to -4.429 points. Post-train block-bootstrap probability of a nonpositive increment was 55%-59%. Even the favorable full-history, incomplete five-challenger family adjustment produced p=0.4071, 0.3595, 0.3448, 0.3395, and 0.3112 at 1-, 5-, 10-, 20-, and 60-session blocks; this lower bound excludes the earlier 642-, 135-, and 2,025-candidate Winter searches. The fixed-core/equal hypothesis is therefore frozen only for deterministic research review.

A naive 0.75% deadband appeared to add roughly 0.64 percentage points of full-period return. That result was invalid because it could suppress small UNG exits and paired index risk reductions. Under the corrected allocation-atomic policy, 0.75% instead changed full return by -0.187 points, validation by -0.104 points, retrospective evaluation by -0.045 points, and the 2025-onward slice by -0.175 points; it reduced turnover by only 0.742x and costs by about 0.011 points. A 1.00% deadband changed full return by -0.153 points and was also negative in recent, validation, and retrospective-evaluation slices while leaving nearly 1% per-leg and 2.37% whole-allocation residuals. Only the stress-cost full history favored 1.00%, by 0.107 points, while every validation, retrospective, and recent comparison remained negative. The production threshold therefore remains 0.25%. Exact threshold selection from this small, non-smooth effect would exceed the requested overfit tolerance.

The broad-search winners improve several retrospective metrics, but none clears a complete-family multiple-testing test. Every challenger was evaluated after the historical evaluation periods were visible. They are rejected for promotion.

## Material audit findings

1. **Historical holdouts are not sealed.** Summer's labeled holdout begins 2025-01-01 and Winter's begins 2025-11-01, while repository strategy development first appears on 2026-06-11. Candidate reports disclose holdout metrics, so later family choices cannot be proven independent of them.
2. **The effective search universe is understated.** Current reality checks cover only the surviving families, not the historical architecture and parameter searches. The all-year family size of one also omits component selection.
3. **A latent Summer sizing path used same-day information.** Volatility-target candidates included the current close-to-close return in volatility used for the position effective at that day's open. The selected fixed-size candidate is unaffected, but the candidate path is noncausal until corrected.
4. **Risk-adjusted ranking was misstated.** Sortino used the standard deviation of negative observations instead of downside deviation over all sessions.
5. **Side gates do not isolate gas alpha.** Some thesis profitability checks credit blended portfolio/index return and can count a row in more than one Winter thesis. These gates should eventually use marginal net gas-sleeve contribution clustered by independent forecast episode.
6. **Summer 2026 evidence is missing, not flat.** The cooling-season day-7 GFS/GEFS calendars end 2025-09-30. The audited daily coverage contract finds 833 required issue dates, 758 fully covered dates, and a 75-date gap per source from 2026-04-24 through 2026-07-07. May-July 2026 rows silently fall back to the index, so the public curve contains no 2026 Summer overlays.
7. **Research/live target parity was broken in Winter.** The prior replay produced 15 mismatches in 1,362 rows, including sign-changing differences on 2024-11-25 and 2025-11-24. The causes were duplicated research/live target logic, a non-canonical storage-season boundary, and incomplete production-source provenance. AIGFS still lacks train/validation history, and research still assumes the adjusted open while live market orders necessarily arrive later.
8. **The broker boundary accepted excess UNG concentration.** Before remediation, a provenance-valid handoff could specify `abs(gasPosition)` up to 1 even though the reviewed seasonal contracts cap the Summer and Winter overlays far below that.
9. **The deadband could suppress safety actions.** Before remediation, the same generic threshold applied to routine drift and small UNG exits, direction changes, or exposure reductions. That made both research accounting and live reconciliation capable of preserving risk the target had explicitly removed.
10. **Short execution evidence is incomplete.** The baseline assumes zero borrow cost; live shortability checks help, but retrospective availability, rejects, and fills are not modeled.
11. **Two forecast atoms contained physically impossible temperatures.** All 18 GFS locations for issue 2022-11-30, target 2022-12-10, lead 10 were near -459F, producing a -495.807F weighted anomaly; all 18 GEFS-mean locations for issue 2026-02-12, target 2026-02-13, lead 1 were -459.67F, producing a -491.868F anomaly. These are missing/corrupt-data sentinels, not weather. They previously entered forecast scoring and weather QA. Removing the two atomic score rows and their 36 location rows corrects the joined weather-QA result to RMSE 5.63F and R2 0.374 over 18,366 observations.
12. **The Winter source score is non-stationary.** Thirty-one of 37 train follow events were formed mostly from GFS and GEFS, while validation and holdout events typically combined four to six sources; AIGFS has no train or validation history. Taking a maximum source score and increasing confidence with source count mechanically rewards later periods for having more models. This is why a fixed source core is a sensible research hypothesis, but its negative post-train result prevents promotion.
13. **The return evidence is concentrated and cost-sensitive.** The selection-prefix active-return sum is 24.878 points; the top day supplies 22%, the top three 59%, and the top five 89%, with only 2021 and 2023 contributing material edge. An illustrative 25 bps UNG/5 bps index cost model plus 10% borrow makes prefix annualized active edge -0.139%; 50/10 bps plus 20% borrow lowers it to -7.467%. Paper slippage and real short-borrow evidence are therefore economic gates, not operational formalities.
14. **A volumetric map is already approximated, but sample size limits the model class.** The reviewed 18-location basket preserves spatial breadth and load weights and derives weighted temperature anomaly, HDD/CDD demand, and extreme-location coverage. A next shadow may preregister a few low-dimensional features such as load-weighted HDD/CDD, breadth, and a physically defined freeze-off proxy. A CNN or other high-capacity map model is not justified by 52 independent Summer heat-follow episodes and would sharply raise overfit risk.

## Implemented audit response

- No historical return winner is promoted and no live signal parameter is changed.
- The current historical evaluation is explicitly labeled development-contaminated and a prospective-evidence seal becomes a required promotion input.
- Same-day volatility lookahead and Sortino calculation defects are corrected with regression tests.
- Paper/live handoffs are constrained at both sanitized handoff and broker boundaries to the exact reviewed component/window/thesis caps: Summer heat follow 0.4375, cold follow 0.35, cold-signal reversion 0.35, heat-signal reversion 0.50, Winter follow/reversion 0.5625, and index fallback zero.
- Summer promotion requires the exact reviewed 18-location forecast basket and weights on every required GFS/GEFS issue date; partial or substituted location coverage fails closed.
- Every forecast location now passes a shared, deliberately broad -150F to 160F plausibility range, finite-value checks, and a 0.01F anomaly-consistency check. One bad location invalidates its complete source/issue/target/lead atom. Live inference rejects such an atom and fails closed; calendar construction, historical optimizers, QA, and parity replay quarantine both the atomic location set and matching score row and preserve diagnostics. The two known corrupt atoms are removed from calculations without hand-editing a performance curve.
- Winter research and live inference now call one pure target engine. Storage-season state uses the canonical September boundary, and selected forecast/source provenance is derived from the reviewed contract. Deterministic production-source replay still matches Summer 585/585 and Winter 1,362/1,362 targets—1,947/1,947 combined—with zero gas-position, index-fraction, or thesis mismatches after weather quarantine. The seven-source Winter replay accepts 18,387 of 18,389 score rows after quarantining the two invalid atoms. Selected strategy IDs and the material target fields—gas position, index fraction, and thesis—remain unchanged.
- The research simulator and Alpaca reconciler now share one versioned deadband policy. The 0.25% threshold still suppresses immaterial same-direction drift, but UNG entries, exits, direction reversals, UNG target risk reductions, reductions paired with any executable gross increase, and any reductions from an over-deployed portfolio bypass it. If an over-deployed account cannot place a valid reducing order, reconciliation blocks instead of reporting a benign no-op.
- The strategy seal now binds a canonical, fixed-point artifact core rather than a cyclic digest of mutable generation/evidence status. Missing or mismatched strategy-contract or artifact-core seals block paper and live routing, and every prospective observation binds both. The reviewed strategy digest is `1bc910fe30f7f0e3f161f017fe0d7a05f2aad8e0b0a9fa1193db58dfd297d3ec`; the artifact-core digest is `413f2854fdf1274caf015c919b91e5bdecb0c862b8f8885415c5487bc85593cb`.
- The 1.50%-2.00% Summer fade ramp is frozen as research-only shadow logic under its own digest and a digest of the complete active Summer comparator contract. It is excluded from candidate selection, promotion, selected ledgers, the executable contract, dashboard DTOs, and broker handoffs; `npm run research:summer-shadow` can write one owner-only, append-only, structurally validated record before 09:30 New York time only on the active Summer comparator schedule. Winter-only sessions, weekends, published full-day closures, and dates beyond the versioned 2026-2028 calendar fail closed, and those records cannot unlock paper or live routing.
- The fixed-core/equal Winter idea is captured in a digested, execution-ineligible challenger family with a deterministic evaluator against the unchanged active comparator. It is research-only: it cannot alter selected artifacts, live target inference, parity, browser DTOs, broker handoffs, or paper/live eligibility, and its historical result is explicitly development-contaminated. Reproduce the JSON report on standard output with `npm run research:winter-shadow`.
- Prospective claims now require an observation end, sealed-contract and evidence digests, feasible completed-season chronology, and ordered reviews/approvals.
- Live eligibility now requires contract-bound, pseudonymized Alpaca paper evidence covering at least 60 trading sessions and 10 fills—including four UNG fills with at least two long and two short—with reviewed median absolute slippage at or below 25 bps and p95 at or below 50 bps.
- The end-to-end seal now includes the exact Summer location universe and a canonical Alpaca execution/risk profile tied to the backtest's 98% deployment, 0.25% deadband, and 80/20 VOO/QQQM basket. Production environment drift blocks before broker access, and test artifacts are confined to confirmed loopback endpoints.
- Evidence and approvals are checked against the runtime clock; future-dated records and reviews made before a claimed period is complete cannot unlock routing. The stable paper-account pseudonym stays out of browser-imported artifacts.
- The strongest simple Winter idea is documented as a rejected retrospective challenger. If revisited, it must be frozen before observation and evaluated without further tuning.

## Required evidence before promotion

Freeze a single end-to-end contract and candidate-registry digest before collecting new evidence. The next evaluation must use timestamped inference, quotes, shortability/borrow state, routing decisions, rejects, and Alpaca paper fills. The checked-in seal requires at least 60 independent episodes, two complete Summers, and two complete Winters. Before live approval it also requires at least 60 paper-trading sessions and 10 fills, including four UNG fills with at least two long and two short, with reviewed median absolute slippage at or below 25 bps and p95 at or below 50 bps. Evaluation must also preserve a purge/embargo covering forecast lead plus holding period, report parameter-neighborhood and block-length sensitivity, and evaluate marginal gas contribution net of costs.

Any change to signal, sizing, source set, location universe, cost model, selection rule, or broker execution/risk profile invalidates the seal and restarts the prospective window. Until those requirements and a separately reviewed live approval are present, permitted use is research and shadow observation only.
